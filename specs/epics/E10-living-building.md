# E10 — Living building

Status: done

## Outcome

The building feels inhabited all day. Several projects work on their own floors, light changes through the day, and agents do small things between tasks. Everything is still simulated and still derived from the snapshot.

## Why after E09

E09 makes one floor complete and honest. E10 adds delight on top. None of it may change a workflow rule or make a panel lie.

## Stories

- [S10-01](../stories/S10-01-multi-project-floors.md) Multi-project floors
- [S10-02](../stories/S10-02-day-and-ambient-life.md) Time of day and ambient life
- [S10-03](../stories/S10-03-quality-tiers.md) Quality tiers and screen glow

## Done when

- At least two seeded projects occupy two floors, and every view filters by floor without a second store.
- Ambient motion stops under reduced motion and never changes presence.
- The office holds 50+ fps on a mid-range laptop at the default tier.

## Out of scope

Real runtimes (E11), sound that plays without a user gesture, and seasonal decoration packs.
